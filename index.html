Getting started

Installing BSMS

Download once, install like any other program. Pick your computer's type below.

Download the Mac version

Go to the Downloads page and open the newest release at the top. Choose mac-arm64 if your Mac is from 2020 or later (Apple Silicon / M-series), or mac-x64 for an older Intel Mac. Not sure which? Click the Apple menu (the icon at the top-left corner of your screen) → About This Mac — it tells you.

Open the downloaded file and install

Double-click the downloaded .dmg file, then drag the BSMS icon into the Applications folder shown.

Open BSMS for the first time

Go to Applications and double-click BSMS. Since this app isn't yet registered with Apple, you'll see a warning the very first time — this is expected, not a virus. Right-click (or Control-click) the BSMS icon and choose Open, then confirm in the dialog that appears. Every time after this, it opens normally with a plain double-click.

Download the Windows version

Go to the Downloads page and open the newest release at the top. Download the file ending in windows-setup.exe.

Run the installer

Double-click the downloaded file. Windows will show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen — this is expected for a new app that isn't registered with Microsoft yet, not a virus warning. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Follow the setup steps

The installer will ask where to install BSMS — the suggested location is fine, just click through. A shortcut will appear on your Desktop and Start Menu when it's done.

BSMS isn't code-signed yet, which is why both operating systems show a warning on first install. This is normal — once you've opened it the one time as described above, it will never ask again.

Getting started

The first time you open it

BSMS walks you through a short setup the very first time — after that, you just log in.

Tell it about your showroom

Name, address, contact number, and your GST details (GSTIN, PAN, state). If you don't have your GST number handy right now, you can skip it and add it later from Settings — you just won't be able to bill GST invoices until it's filled in.

Pick your GST rates

BSMS comes with the standard Indian GST rates ready to go (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%), with the usual two-wheeler rate pre-selected. You can add more rates later if you need them.

Create your own login

Your name, phone number, and a password. This becomes the Owner account — the one with full access to everything, including adding other staff logins later.

This setup only happens once. Every time after this, opening BSMS takes you straight to a login screen — and everything you enter stays saved on this computer, even after updates.

Overview

What BSMS can do

Everything a two-wheeler showroom needs day to day, organised into a few simple areas.

Customers & bikes

Keep a record of every customer and the bikes they own. Find anyone in seconds by searching their name or phone number.

Service tickets

Track a bike from the moment it comes in — the complaint, the estimate, what parts and labour were actually used — right through to delivery.

Inventory & stock

Track exactly how much of every part you have, scan barcodes, and get warned automatically when something is running low.

Billing & GST

Create proper GST invoices with CGST/SGST calculated automatically. Accept part-payments and always know exactly who owes what.

Sale orders

Take a booking or an advance order before the final sale — nothing affects your stock or GST numbers until it's actually converted into a bill.

Suppliers & purchases

Record what you buy from suppliers and what you've paid them. Your stock updates on its own as soon as a purchase bill is recorded.

Accounts & ledger

A simple, always-up-to-date running total of money in and out, plus a profit & loss view — no accounting background needed.

Reports

Daily business summaries, a list of who still owes you money, and GST reports ready for filing.

Staff, attendance & roles

Track who's working and when. Give each staff member their own login that only shows them what they need.

Audit trail

Every correction — a stock adjustment, a rewritten invoice, a cancelled payment — is logged with who did it and why, so nothing important gets lost.

Backup & restore

Download a complete copy of your data anytime, and restore it back in one click if you ever need to — see Backups below.

In practice

A typical day with BSMS

The easiest way to understand what BSMS does is to see how a normal day actually flows through it.

Morning

Staff check in

Each staff member checks in for the day. You can see at a glance who's in and correct anyone's timing yourself if needed.

A customer drops off a bike

Open a Service Ticket

Note the bike, the customer, and what's wrong. Give them an estimate if they'd like one before work starts.

In the workshop

Technician does the work

The technician records exactly which parts were used and what labour charges apply — this is what actually gets billed later, not the earlier estimate, so the customer is always charged for the real work done.

Bike is ready

Bill the customer

The invoice is generated straight from the parts and labour actually recorded, with GST calculated for you. The customer pays by cash, UPI, card, or bank transfer — even a part-payment is tracked correctly.

Meanwhile at the counter

A walk-in buys a spare part

Not every sale needs a service ticket — a standalone sale bills a customer directly and still updates stock and GST correctly.

Bike goes home

Mark it delivered

The ticket is marked delivered, recording who handed the bike back and when.

End of day

Check the dashboard

A quick look shows today's sales, what's still owed to you, and anything running low in stock — no digging required.

Staff access

Staff & roles

Every staff member gets their own login, and only sees the parts of BSMS relevant to their job. One person can hold more than one role — a working owner, for instance, typically has all of them.

RoleWhat they can do
OwnerEverything — including adding staff, correcting mistakes, seeing all reports and accounts, and activating the license.
TechnicianWorks on service tickets and records the parts and labour actually used.
CashierHandles billing, standalone sales, purchases, and recording payments.
DeliveryMarks finished bikes as delivered to the customer.
AuditorRead-only access to reports, accounts, and the audit trail — for an accountant or a trusted reviewer.

Licensing

Trial & activation

BSMS is free to try for 7 days from the moment you first open it — no card, no signup, nothing to enter up front.

During your trial

A small banner at the top of the app shows how many days are left. Everything works normally — nothing is limited.

Activating

Once you have an activation key, go to Settings → License and paste it in. You don't have to wait for the trial to run out.

If the trial runs out

You'll see a simple "Activate BSMS" screen when you open the app. Paste your key there to continue — your data is completely safe and untouched the whole time.

Being locked out never deletes anything. The moment a valid key is entered, you're straight back to exactly where you left off.

Your data

Backups — please read this one

BSMS keeps all your data on this computer only — nothing is sent anywhere else. That's good for privacy and means it works with no internet connection, but it also means you're responsible for backing it up.

Download a backup regularly

Go to Settings → Showroom Profile and click Download backup. This saves one file with your entire business — customers, bikes, tickets, invoices, everything.

Keep that file somewhere safe

Copy it to a USB drive, email it to yourself, or upload it to Google Drive — anywhere other than only on this one computer.

Restoring, if you ever need to

On the same screen, Restore from backup lets you load a backup file back in — useful if you move to a new computer or something goes wrong. This completely replaces whatever is currently in the app with the backup, so use it carefully.

If this computer is ever lost, stolen, or badly damaged and you don't have a recent backup saved elsewhere, that data cannot be recovered. A backup taken once a week (or after a busy day) is enough for most showrooms.

Staying current

Updating to a new version

When a new version is available, download and install it from the Downloads page exactly the same way you installed the first time — it installs right over the existing app. Your data, your login, and your activation all carry over automatically. No need to back up first, though it never hurts.
